    Worth knowing

    • The pass activates on first use, either at your first cultural site or when you pick up the bus ticket.
    • One visit per site. Each place on the list can be used once.
    • Show the pass to staff before you go in. They may check it is valid.
    • Buses are free. On your first ride the driver gives you a printed ticket with its own QR code. Keep that ticket, it covers every ride after that and it cannot be reissued if lost.
    • The pass itself only scans once on a bus. After that it is the paper ticket you show.
