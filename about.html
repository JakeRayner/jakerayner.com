About

Over 10 Years Designing Products Used by Millions.

I'm a UX and Product Design Lead based in Manchester, with over 10 years' experience designing digital products across automotive, e-commerce, fintech and online gaming, including roles at Aston Martin Lagonda and Bentley Motors.

For the past three years I've led UX at Debenhams Group, setting up the UX function after the Debenhams acquisition and scaling it across all 21 brands in the group, designing industry-leading experiences while optimising for technical synergies and high-velocity delivery.

I lead teams and I stay hands-on. In practice that means setting design direction, building the systems and standards a team works to, and raising UX maturity in organisations that haven't had it before. It also means being in the file, in the research session, and increasingly in the code.

Outside work I'm a musician, photographer and videographer, and a car enthusiast, which explains a fair amount of my career.

How I work

My Values.

Positive Culture, Outstanding Outcomes

I believe that a positive, trusting and respectful culture has a positive effect on the way a design team functions and the products/experiences it creates. I always strive to encourage and facilitate a positive culture, a strong team mentality, and openly listen to, mentor, and support others.

Keep It Simple, Make It Happen

The best UX is invisible. It's simple, intuitive and just works. I like to help create tangible experiences that are innovative, reduce complexity and meet the needs of both users and the business, whilst being simple and easy to use.

Research, Design, Innovate and Validate

UX, UI, and Product design is a broad vista of disciplines, skills, and processes. From discovery research, customer journey mapping, and information architecture, through to pixel-perfect UI, high-fidelity prototyping, motion design, and much more.

In my experience, UX, UI, and Product Design can mean different things, in different organisations, at different times. Some are more focused on visually stunning deliverables and well-architected design systems, whilst others are more focused on identifying user needs, pain points and friction in their customer journeys. All of this has its right place and time in each individual organisation.

However, I feel design is at its best when all of these disciplines, skills and processes work together. With the right people focused on the right disciplines, effectively working together as a team, amazing products and experiences can be created.

Someone with a good eye for visual design, who keeps up to date with product design trends, can bring a lot of value to a design team. At the same time, you need great problem solvers and researchers to ensure that you understand users' needs, wants and frustrations, creating informed and validated designs. When each individual component of a design operation is effectively coordinated, the outcomes are far greater than the sum of their parts.
