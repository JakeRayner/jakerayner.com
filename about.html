About

Ten years designing products used by millions.

I'm a UX, UI and Product Design Lead based in Manchester. Over the past ten years I've designed and delivered digital products for globally recognised organisations across automotive, retail, finance and betting, working on everything from in-car infotainment to mobile banking to retail at scale.

I lead teams and I stay hands-on. In practice that means setting design direction, building the systems and standards a team works to, and raising UX maturity in organisations that haven't had it before. It also means being in the file, in the research session, and increasingly in the code.

Outside work I'm a musician, photographer and videographer, and a car enthusiast, which explains a fair amount of my career.

How I work

Culture, craft, and getting it shipped.

Positive culture, outstanding outcomes

A trusting, respectful culture has a direct effect on what a design team produces. I mentor, listen, and build that deliberately, because it shows up in the work.

Keep it simple, make it happen

The best UX is invisible. Simple, intuitive, and it works. Reducing complexity while meeting the needs of both the user and the business.

Research, design, validate

Discovery research and journey mapping through to pixel perfect UI and high-fidelity prototyping. Tested, not assumed.
